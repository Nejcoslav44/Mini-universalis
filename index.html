Photogrammetry Capture

This site uses your phone camera to capture a full image set for a chair or other object. It does not build a SolidWorks-ready mesh in pure browser code; instead it gives you a clean capture workflow and exports your image set so you can reconstruct the mesh in photogrammetry software.

Browser-only HTML is great for capture. High-quality mesh reconstruction still normally needs a dedicated engine such as RealityCapture, Meshroom, COLMAP, Polycam, or RealityScan.

How To Scan a Chair

  1. Keep the chair still and use even light.
  2. Make one full circle at seat height.
  3. Make a second circle slightly above, angled down.
  4. Make a third circle slightly below, angled up.
  5. Keep 70% overlap between shots.
  6. Take extra close shots of seams and cushion edges.
